Proof of Concept Analysis: Which Experience Fits Best?

✨ Option A: 3D Pop-Out & Screen Fit

  • Physics: Tiles blast forward along the Z-axis directly toward the user's screen.
  • Transition: Fluidly gathers and fits into an organized, floating glassmorphic modal.
  • Best For: High conversion rate — users immediately see all 12 furniture models clearly with prices/WhatsApp inquiry buttons without leaving the page.
  • Performance: Lightweight 60fps CSS 3D transforms, works smoothly on all devices.

🚪 Option B: Dive-In Portal (Room Entry)

  • Physics: First-person camera dive-in zooming directly through the open wooden doors.
  • Transition: Wardrobe frame expands 10x off-screen as the user "walks into" a luxury showroom room.
  • Best For: High "WOW" factor and storytelling — creates a magical Narnia / luxury flagship store feeling.
  • Interaction: 3D cylindrical carousel inside that users can spin 360 degrees.
